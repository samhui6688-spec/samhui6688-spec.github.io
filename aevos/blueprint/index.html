01

定位：一個入口，對接成個平台The Platform in One View

唔係單一功能，係一個以長期健康管理為核心嘅大健康平台。 Not a single feature — a big-health platform built around long-term health management.

長期健康管理Long-term Health Management · Core

平台嘅核心：持續跟進每一位客戶嘅健康，唔係一年先驗一次身。 The core of the platform — your health, followed continuously, not once a year.

AI 健康助手AI Health Agents

隨時問、隨時答；識得睇住你嘅數據，識得幾時要提你。 Ask anytime. Agents that watch your data and know when to speak up.

Aevos App
唯一入口 · ONE ENTRY

身體探索Body Explorer

互動式 3D 身體，一問即飛去相關器官講解 — 平台嘅入門體驗。 An interactive 3D body that flies to the organ you ask about — the front door of the platform.

健康數據紀錄Health Data Record

你嘅健康數據長期保存、持續累積，成為日後所有服務嘅基礎。 Your health record, kept and growing over time — the foundation every service builds on.

用戶只需要嚟我哋個 App，就對接晒。 One app, everything connected.

03

發展藍圖The Roadmap

初步設計 · INITIAL DESIGN — 以下階段為規劃方向，可隨時調整，並非已推出服務 The phases below are an initial, evolving plan — direction that may change, not yet live services. We will keep refining it to raise the quality.

第一期 · PHASE 1

AI 健康管理平台AI Health Management Platform

進行中 · NOW

平台嘅地基：AI 健康助手、長期健康紀錄、互動身體探索，全部喺同一個 App 入面。 The foundation: AI health agents, long-term health records and the interactive body explorer, in one app.

會員得到：一個長期跟住自己健康嘅 AI 助手，同一個隨時查得到嘅身體探索入口。 Members get: an AI agent that follows their health over time, and a body they can explore by simply asking.
第二期 · PHASE 2

AI × 中醫AI × Traditional Chinese Medicine

規劃中 · NEXT

AI 輔助中醫：體質分析、調理方案 — 中西合璧嘅健康管理，調理同監測並行。 AI-assisted TCM: constitution analysis and conditioning plans — east-meets-west health management, conditioning alongside monitoring.

會員得到：按自己體質度身嘅調理方向，唔再靠估。 Members get: conditioning guidance shaped by their own constitution — no more guesswork.
第三期 · PHASE 3

跨境醫療Cross-border Medical Services

規劃中 · PLANNED

一個 App 預約同協調跨境醫療服務，令優質醫療更容易接觸、成本更合理。 One app to book and coordinate medical care across borders — quality care, easier to reach and far more affordable than overseas options.

會員得到：由預約到跟進，一個入口搞掂，唔使自己四圍搵、四圍對。 Members get: booking through follow-up handled in one place — no running around, no guesswork.
第四期 · PHASE 4

自家產品Own Products

規劃中 · PLANNED

由平台嘅研發同數據出發，自家研發護膚同調理產品 — 管理、服務、產品，成條鏈自己做。 Skincare and wellness products developed from the platform's own R&D and data — management, services and products on one chain.

會員得到：用緊嘅產品，係按平台累積嘅真實健康數據研發出嚟，唔係齋賣貨。 Members get: products grounded in real health data the platform has gathered — not shelf stock.
第五期 · PHASE 5

實體網絡Physical Network

規劃中 · PLANNED

實體店同合作醫療機構採用 Aevos 嘅儀器同系統；線下接觸點越多，數據飛輪轉得越快。 Stores and partner medical institutions running Aevos devices and systems — every physical touchpoint feeds the data flywheel.

會員得到：線上線下同一份健康紀錄，去到邊都接得返。 Members get: one health record that follows them, online and in person.
第六期 · PHASE 6

大健康平台The Big-Health Platform

願景 · VISION

開放平台：其他健康系統、服務同產品都可以接入嚟 — 用戶只需要一個 App，就對接到成個大健康世界。 An open platform other health systems connect into — one app opening onto the whole world of health.

會員得到：唔使逐個服務開帳戶、逐樣嘢重新填資料，一個入口做晒好多嘢。 Members get: no separate accounts for every service, no re-entering the same details — one entry, many doors.
04

商業閉環The Closed Loop

每一環都餵返下一環 — 轉得越多，越啱你。 Every turn of the loop feeds the next — and makes it more personal.

1

健康數據Health Data

會員嘅健康數據長期累積，唔再散落喺唔同地方。Members' health data, gathered and kept in one place over time.

2

AI 分析AI Insight

AI 將數據變成睇得明、用得到嘅建議同預警。AI turns data into guidance and early warnings people can act on.

3

服務Services

醫療同中醫調理，按分析結果對接，唔使自己估邊樣啱。Medical care and TCM conditioning, matched to the insight — no guessing what fits.

4

產品Products

護膚同調理產品由研發而來，效果同反饋再變成新數據。Skincare and wellness products born of R&D — their results become new data.

5

更多數據Richer Data

數據越豐富，下一輪分析越準 — 成個循環自己推動自己。Richer data sharpens the next round of insight — the loop drives itself.
