Questions teachers ask

How do teachers get started?

We agree the curriculum topic and check students’ prior knowledge and classroom devices. Teacher orientation, a practice run and in-class support can be discussed; delivery follows the agreed scope.

Does the school need a lab?

A computer-only lesson has no physical experiment. It typically needs a suitable browser, an internet connection and platform accounts. Wet-lab activities are planned separately after checking facilities, equipment, safety procedures and supervision.

How is learning assessed?

We suggest worksheets, student work and teacher feedback. Each module ends with a student outcome teachers can assess.

How are costs worked out?

Costs depend on student numbers, lesson time, platform access and instructor support. Materials and venue for wet-lab work are agreed separately.

What language are the materials in?

The current sample booklets are in English. Ask about Chinese-language versions when you enquire.

About the EDB AI funding programme

Under the Education Bureau’s AI for Empowering Learning and Teaching Funding Programme, successful publicly funded school applicants receive a one-off HK$500,000 grant for AI-assisted learning and teaching, used according to the guidelines and each school’s plan.
